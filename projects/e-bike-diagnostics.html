01 — Graduation project

Hearing motor faults without opening the motor

A vibration-based diagnostic tool for e-bike motors, so they can be repaired instead of replaced.

ContextMSc Graduation
WhereTU Delft
Year2026
OutcomePaper · IWAR 2026
Fig. 2 — Prototype photo

Problem

When an e-bike motor fails, it usually gets replaced as a whole. Finding out what is actually wrong means opening it up, which costs time and makes repair the less attractive option.

Approach

I disassembled e-bike motors to map their architecture and understand where faults occur.

Then I built a working sensor prototype that collects vibration data from running motors, so healthy and defective motors can be compared.

Result

The prototype showed that vibration analysis can detect motor defects without opening the motor. The paper on this work was accepted for presentation at IWAR 2026.
