About

Hands + head

I like understanding how products work by taking them apart. At eighteen I bought a fifty-year-old Zündapp moped without knowing how a two-stroke engine worked. I've since spent more time repairing it than riding it.

That curiosity runs through my work: from redesigning a tool and taking it to production at KUBO's R&D department, to packaging concepts at Click-Pax and my graduation project on repairable e-bike motors.
