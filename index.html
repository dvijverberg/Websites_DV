About

Naaldwijk, NL

I like understanding how products work by taking them apart. At eighteen I bought a fifty-year-old Zündapp moped without knowing how a two-stroke engine worked. I've since spent more time repairing it than riding it.

I still work that way. I'd rather build something rough and test it than keep drawing. These days that's as often software as hardware: I write my own Blender add-ons and Python tools, with AI as a working partner.
