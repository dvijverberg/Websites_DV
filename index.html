About

De Lier, NL

I like understanding how products work by taking them apart. At eighteen I bought a fifty-year-old Zündapp moped without knowing how a two-stroke engine worked. I've since spent more time repairing it than riding it.

I still work that way. I'd rather build a rough version and test it than keep drawing. That goes for a sensor setup on a workbench, a tool that has to be made by a supplier, or a piece of software that saves a team hours of repetitive work.

More and more of what I build is digital. I write my own Blender add-ons and Python tools, and I use AI as a working partner to get from idea to a working version quickly.
